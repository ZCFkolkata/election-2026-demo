Election Information

Election Overview

Zomi Christian Fellowship Kolkata will conduct elections for the Executive Committee and Women's Wing for the term 2027–2029.

Voter Eligibility

All individuals included in the official voter list are eligible to participate in both the Executive Committee and Women's Wing elections.

Voting Guidelines

  • Each eligible voter can vote in both sections.
  • One candidate can be selected for each position.
  • A selected candidate cannot be selected for another position within the same section.
  • Each voter can submit one complete ballot per section.

Voting Limit

Each eligible voter is permitted a maximum of two ballot submissions: one for the Executive Committee and one for the Women's Wing.

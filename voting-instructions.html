Voter Instructions

Guidelines for participating in the ZCFK Election 2026

1. Voter Eligibility

Only individuals included in the official eligible voter list are permitted to participate in the election.

2. Voter Verification

Visit the Voter Portal and enter your assigned Voter ID and Verification PIN.

3. Election Sections

Eligible voters can participate in both election sections:

  • Executive Committee – 8 positions.
  • Women's Wing – 6 positions.

4. Selecting Candidates

  • Select one candidate for each position.
  • Choose a different candidate for each position within the same section.
  • All positions must be completed before proceeding.

5. Reviewing Your Ballot

Carefully review your selections before submitting your ballot. Make sure your choices are correct.

6. Voting Limit

Each eligible voter may submit one ballot for the Executive Committee and one ballot for the Women's Wing, subject to the official election rules.

7. Important Reminder

Please vote responsibly and respect the confidentiality and integrity of the election process.
